We've all been there—looking at the messy code we just threw together, thinking, “I'll clean this up someday.”
We feel kinda relieved when it works, even if it’s ugly. Better something than nothing, right?
And we all tell ourselves we’ll come back later to fix it.
But of course, on days like that, we forget about LeBlanc’s Law:

“Later” means “never.”
